FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I receive my product?

After payment, send your customer to your SellAuth delivery flow. Update the product link and delivery instructions to match your setup.

Do I need coding experience?

No. The goal is to make setup straightforward. Your exact installation steps can be placed inside your SellAuth product delivery message.

Can you make a custom bot?

Yes. Use the contact section to collect a Discord username and a short description of what you want built.

How do I connect my SellAuth store?

Replace the demo SellAuth links in index.html with your actual product checkout URLs. The site is otherwise static and GitHub Pages friendly.
